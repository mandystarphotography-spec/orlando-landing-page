How to use this with your page

  1. Open your landing page file and find the photo you want to adjust (search for its filename).
  2. Copy its URL from between the quotes in background-image: url('...') and paste it above.
  3. Pick the crop shape that matches that spot (most are the portrait ones).
  4. Click around the preview until it looks right, then hit Copy.
  5. Paste the copied line right after that photo's background-image line in the file (or replace its existing background-position line if there is one), save, and refresh your browser.
